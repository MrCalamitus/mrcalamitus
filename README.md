## What I do

I design and lead the build of **cloud-native systems that run real operations** — regulatory platforms, electronic credential issuance, election-scale infrastructure — and I bring **generative AI into production** where it moves business metrics.

- 🏗️ **Architect & CTO** — end-to-end ownership: architecture, security, infra cost (CFO mindset) and team velocity
- 🤖 **Applied AI** — multi-agent pipelines (CrewAI), RAG, local LLMs (Ollama), AI video & voice (HeyGen, ElevenLabs)
- 🔐 **Security by design** — MSc in Security & IT Engineering (IPN, 2024) · BSc Computer Engineering (ESIME Culhuacán) · AWS Cloud Practitioner · compliance-heavy domains (health, education, elections)
- 👥 **Leadership** — I upskill teams on AI-assisted development and own the roadmap, not just the code

### 🗳️ Civic-tech & high-stakes systems

Through IPN collaboration agreements I've worked on systems where **failure is not an option** — national elections and health regulation:

- **INE (Instituto Nacional Electoral)** — functional testing of the federal election IT systems (2015, 2017–2018), technical verification of **electronic voting machines** (Coahuila/Hidalgo 2020, Aguascalientes/Tamaulipas 2022) and the **security audit of the Internet voting system** for Mexicans abroad (SIVEI, 2022).
- **IEEM (Estado de México)** — designed a **real-time code review model for the PREP** (preliminary results program) before, during and after election day (2021).
- **COFEPRIS** — digitalization platforms for **clinical trials** (2023) and **health registries** (2024).
- **Colegio de Notarios de la CDMX** — architected the *Red Integral Notarial*, a technology innovation platform for the notary network (2018–2020, ~1,500 commits).

🎤 **Speaker** — 9th Information Security Congress (2021): workshop *"Security practices for microservices on AWS"*.

🧪 **AI Lab — Hermes.** A self-hosted personal assistant on a local LLM: summarizes my WhatsApp groups, tracks monthly expenses and scouts daily news, references and dev opportunities. Running in production for one user: me.
